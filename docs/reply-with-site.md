[LINK]

Both shows are in there and searchable. Type a guest, a topic, a phrase — the
results narrow as you go — then filter by show or year and click through to the
episode.

Two things turned out better than I expected:

**It searches what was actually said.** It turns out your feed already publishes
transcripts for most episodes, so search reaches inside the audio, not just the
show notes. Searching "picket line" brings up 59 episodes — only two of which
mention it in the written notes. And when your words were spoken aloud, the
result shows the exact times; click one and the episode plays from that second.

**It spots repeats.** The archive flags when the same programme has aired more
than once, either as an encore or on both shows, and says so under the episode.
It found 22 encores and 5 programmes that ran on both — which I think is the
"have we run this already" problem Chris described.

There's also a Help button explaining the search options, with examples you can
click to try.
